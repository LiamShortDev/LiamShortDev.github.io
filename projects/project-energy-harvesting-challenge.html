Energy Harvesting Challenge

Introduction

The objective of this project week is to build a simple wind turbine and convert some of the wind energy into electrical energy. 
 
This page demonstrates: 
- The way professional engineering skills were used for time management, teamwork, and social media communication. 
- How data analysis was used to make evidence-based decisions on the wind turbine decide.
- How the impact of electronic devices was considered. 

Project Management

Objectives

The project was divided into several clear stages to ensure organised progress. First, we planned the objectives and gathered required materials. Next, we designed and assembled the system, testing individual components to confirm functionality. Data collection was then carried out under controlled conditions, followed by data processing and analysis using software tools. Finally, we evaluated results, compared them to predictions, and documented conclusions to reflect on performance and potential improvements.

Inclusive teamwork

Skills matrix

Teamwork and Organisation

Our team maintained structure and efficiency through consistent check-ins and open communication. We held regular meetings to track progress, distribute responsibilities, and resolve any obstacles together. Group chats and in-person discussions kept everyone updated and encouraged active participation. Collaboration was further strengthened by working in pairs during practical sessions, allowing us to share skills and learn from one another. This ongoing interaction and supportive environment helped us stay coordinated, motivated, and aligned toward our goal for the energy-harvesting challenge.

Process for Bystander Intervention

To encourage bystander intervention and teamwork within our mini wind turbine project, we used scenario-based discussions. We explored realistic situations that could occur in academic or project settings and applied the “5D’s” approach to intervention. Practicing our responses in a supportive environment helped us build confidence, recognise inappropriate behaviour, and reinforce respect within the group. This proactive method promoted inclusivity, improved communication, and strengthened our ability to act responsibly as a team.

Focus on Protected Characteristics

During the project, I prioritised inclusivity by focusing on age and gender reassignment as protected characteristics. I made sure that everyone, regardless of age or prior experience, was able to contribute meaningfully and take on important responsibilities. I also respected each person’s chosen name, pronouns, and gender identity, ensuring that our team remained respectful and welcoming. This inclusive mindset not only promoted equality but also improved collaboration, trust, and creativity within the group.

Data Analysis and Visualisation

Total Energy vs Wind Speed for Different Setups

Plot Rationale

A line graph was chosen to display the total energy harvested at each wind speed for the different fan configurations, as it effectively displays how energy output changes with increasing wind speed. Although data was not collected between the intervals the tested wind speeds, connecting the points highlights the overall trends and makes comparing the setups more intuitive. This visualisation emphasises the relationship between wind speed and energy generation, making it easier to identify which configuration performs best across the tested range.

Climate and sustainability Impact of electronics devices

Relevant SDG: Goal 7 – Affordable and Clean Energy

This project aligns with UN Sustainable Development Goal 7, which focuses on ensuring access to affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy. By testing different fan configurations to optimise energy harvesting from wind, the project contributes to the development of efficient renewable energy systems. This supports the transition away from fossil fuels toward cleaner, sustainable energy generation.

Electrical System Sustainability

The system operates by converting mechanical energy from wind into electrical energy through a generator connected to rotating fan blades. As wind speed increases, the kinetic energy captured by the fan is transformed into electrical output, which can be measured and analysed. Sustainability is enhanced because this system demonstrates how renewable sources, specifically wind energy, can be harnessed efficiently without producing greenhouse gas emissions. Improving the design and performance of such systems supports cleaner energy technologies and reduces dependence on non-renewable resources.
