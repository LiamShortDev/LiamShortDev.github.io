Key Characteristics of being a good engineer

What does it mean to be an engineer? 

By definition, engineering is the disciplined application of scientific and mathematical principles to problem solve. However, its personal meaning supersedes any definition. It is a commitment to curiosity, keeping up to date with the latest technology and having a genuine interest in expanding your knowledge. It is the resilience, accepting failure at face value and embracing it to fuel the desire to succeed. It is the teamwork, sharing knowledge to both technical and general audiences, playing to your strengths and indicating weaknesses. 

Overall, being an engineer is being able to shape the world around you by mentoring others, creating world-changing technology and embracing those around you.

Why is being an engineer inspirational to me?

Being an engineer inspires me because it allows me to make a real impact. I'm motivate by the idea that engineering isn't just about solving the technical problems, its about seeking them. Seeing an issue in day-to-day life and being able to think of ways I can make something that fixes something, makes it more efficient or creates opportunities that would never otherwise exist. That sense of purpose gives engineering its meaning to me.

How can I connect with other engineers?

Connecting with other engineers is important for both personal and professional development. One of the most effective ways to do this is through professional networking platforms such as LinkedIn, where I can build a profile, showcase my skills, and engage with industry professionals. Joining professional organisations like the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET) or IEEE can also provide access to events, resources, and a wider professional community.

Attending events, seminars, and workshops is another valuable way to meet other engineers and learn from their experiences. These events provide opportunities to discuss ideas, gain insights into current industry trends, and build meaningful connections. Participating in group projects, hackathons, or volunteering activities can also help me collaborate with others and develop teamwork skills while expanding my network.

Finally, maintaining connections is just as important as making them. This can be done by staying in touch, sharing knowledge, and being open to collaboration. By actively engaging with others and contributing to the engineering community, I can build strong professional relationships that will support my career development.
