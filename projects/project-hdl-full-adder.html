Simulation explanation

At the start, all inputs are 0, producing sum=0 and cout=0. As the loop increments from 0 to 7, the statement {a, b, cin} = i cycles through every possible 3-bit input. The waveform shows each signal stepping every 10 ns, matching the #10 delay in the testbench.

The results follow full-adder logic:

sum is 1 when an odd number of inputs are 1 (XOR behaviour)
cout is 1 when at least two inputs are 1 (carry generation)

For example:

a=0, b=1, cin=1 → sum=0, cout=1
a=1, b=1, cin=1 → sum=1, cout=1

Both the waveform and console output confirm correct propagation of intermediate carries (c0, c1) through the two Half Adders. Overall, the simulation demonstrates that the structural design correctly implements a 1-bit Full Adder.
